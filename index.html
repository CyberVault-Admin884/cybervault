Why CYBERVAULT Exists & How To Use

Security is paramount. CYBERVAULT allows verified users to generate unbreakable passwords safely. All data is handled securely, and no passwords are stored.

The 1 KES payment is not optional. It helps:

  • Prevent bots from abusing the system
  • Keep the app ads-free and smooth
  • Support the developer

Step-by-step instructions:

  1. Dial *334# on your phone.
  2. Navigate to Pochi La Biashara.
  3. Send 1 KES to the number 0142122710.
  4. Enter your phone number in the field below (used to identify verified users).
  5. Wait for the voucher verification to be confirmed by the admin.
  6. Enter the voucher received and generate your secure password key.
